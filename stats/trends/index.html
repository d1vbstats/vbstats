Bids by conference

Expected bids, multi-bid contenders dashed line = 1 bid · one-bid leagues are in the table

Projected = teams in the projected bracket above. Expected = sum of each team's chance to make the field, so it counts partial chances (a bubble team at 60% adds 0.6). Seeds = teams seeded 1–16 in the projection. Bubble teams = teams with a 20–80% chance to make the field, with that chance and whether they are in (in) or out (out) of the projected bracket; click the column to sort by how many a league has. AQ favorite = most likely automatic qualifier. Leagues that award the bid through a conference tournament (all but the ACC, Big Ten, Big 12 and WCC) have that tournament simulated: the top teams by conference record are seeded into a bracket and played out with the power ratings, so upsets and bid stealers are part of the odds. Once a tournament is over, its champion is locked in from the results (or name it in data/aq_conferences_2026.csv).

NCAA tournament outlook

Core

SO %
Sideout: share of points won when receiving serve.
PS %
Point scoring: share of points won when serving.
Att eff
Attack efficiency: (kills − errors − blocked) ÷ attempts.
K %
Kills ÷ attempts.
Pass rating
Average reception grade on a 0–3 scale.
Good pass %
Share of receptions graded R# or R+.
UFE
Unforced errors per set: serve + attack + reception + set errors.
Set W %
Share of sets won.

Scorecard

FBSO
First-ball sideout: reception points won before the receiving team's first transition touch. Opponent serve errors count.
ESO
Earned sideout: sideout % with opponent serve errors removed.
xFBSO
Expected FBSO: the D1 first-ball sideout rate for each pass grade, weighted by the team's pass mix. Measures pass quality in sideout terms.
xFBSO (S)
The same, for the opponent's passes off this team's serve. Lower = tougher serve.
GP Eff
First-ball attack efficiency off a good pass (R# / R+).
MP Eff
First-ball attack efficiency off a medium pass (R!).
BP Eff
First-ball attack efficiency off a bad pass (R−).
Tr InSys / Tr OoS
Transition attack efficiency when the possession began on a D# (in system) or D− (out of system) dig.
Tr F
Attack efficiency after receiving a free ball.
KO %
Knockout %: share of serves whose reception was R!, R−, R= (ace) or R/ (overpass).
SE %
Serve error %.
INSABE / OOSABE
Block touch value: (B# + B+ + B! − B− − B=) ÷ (block touches − B/), on attacks whose possession began in system (R#, R+, D#) or out of system (R−, D−). Blank under 5 touches.
Stuffs
Share of opponent attacks blocked for a point.
Create
Kill % on attacks off the team's own dig.
xD K %
Expected kill % off digs: the D1 kill rate for each dig grade, weighted by the team's dig mix. Measures dig quality.

Attacking splits

FBSO swing
An attack in the reception phase (first ball after serve receive).
InSys
FBSO swing off a good pass (R# / R+).
Med
FBSO swing off a medium pass (R!).
OoS
FBSO swing off a bad pass (R−).
Tr
Transition: any attack after the first ball.
Combo
The attack code from the scout file, shown by name: X5 Go · X6 Red · V5 HighZ4 · X1 Tight Quick · V6 HighZ2 · X7 Gap · CF Slide · PP Setter Dump · VP Pipe · XP Bic · XM Push · X2 A · X3 2 Ball · X8 D Ball · X9 Rip · CB B Slide · V8 High D Ball · XS OppSlide.
D1 avg
National efficiency for that combo and phase, shown in grey.
Share
That combo's share of the team's swings in the phase.

Setting

Set att
Set attempts by the player.
Assists
Sets followed by a kill.
Set err %
Share of set attempts graded as errors.
Eff / K % off setter
Attack efficiency and kill % on swings that came off this player's set.
0–1 blk
Share of attacks off the set that met no block, a single blocker or a seam (hole block).

Players

Position
Inferred from touch mix, not a roster: S = mostly sets; L/DS = no attacking or blocking; MB = block-heavy with little reception; OPP/MB = attacks and blocks, no reception; Pin = everyone else.
Recs
Receptions.
Eff / K % off set
For liberos and DS: attacking results on swings off their sets.

Team ratings

Scale
40–99. Each metric is opponent-adjusted (team and opponent effects solved together, so schedule strength is credited), z-scored against D1 teams with 3+ matches, and placed on the scale: up to 80 it follows the normal curve (70 = D1 average), and above 80 it stretches so that 99 takes about 3 standard deviations above average. Only a truly elite team reaches the high 90s.
Attacking
FBSO swing eff, in-system eff, medium eff, out-of-system eff, transition eff.
Serving
xFBSO (S), KO %, ace %, serve error %.
Passing
xFBSO, good pass % (R# / R+), reception error %, pass rating — equally weighted.
Setting
0–1 blocker %, in-system eff off a set, medium-pass eff off a set, set error %.
Blocking
Stuff %, opponent FBSO eff allowed, INSABE, OOSABE, good block touch % (B# + B+ over all block attempts) — equally weighted.
Digging
Dig quality (xD K %), Create, opponent transition eff allowed.
Overall
Weighted blend of the six components; weights are refit each update as the regression of the power net rating on the components.
Pwr #
Power rank. Overall is a skills score and power is a points-won outcome; a gap means over- or under-performing the profile.
▲ ▼ (ratings)
Change from the previous week: every rating is recomputed from the matches through last Sunday, with the same Overall weights, and compared with today's.
Raw / n
On the Team page, grey is the unadjusted season value and n is the number of attempts behind it.

Power rankings & projections

Net
Opponent-adjusted sideout rating minus opponent-adjusted sideout allowed, in points per 100 receptions, with home court removed and small samples regressed to the D1 mean.
SO rating / SO allowed
The two halves of Net.
SOS
Average Net of opponents faced.
Form
Average of the last 10 matches' net sideout relative to what the ratings expected. Positive = playing above rating lately.
Last 10
Record in the last 10 matches (NCAA committee window).
Win prob
From the same ratings: point-win rates receiving and serving → rally-scoring sets (25, win by 2; set 5 to 15) → best of five.
▲ ▼
On the RPI page, the change in rank since the team's previous match. Elsewhere, movement since the previous data update.

Trends

Weekly rank
Power and RPI (with bonuses) recomputed from only the results through each Sunday.
Movers
Change in rank from the chosen week to the latest update. ▲ = moved up.
Stat trends
A team's box-score rates for the matches played each week (Monday–Sunday), its season-to-date line, and the D1 average. Rates are pooled (total kills ÷ total attempts, etc.), not averages of match percentages.
Hot / cold
The chosen stat over the recent window vs every match before it. Opponent efficiency and error rates are flipped so "heating up" always means better.

RPI

RPI
0.25 × win % + 0.50 × opponents' win % + 0.25 × opponents' opponents' win %. D1 games only; opponents' records exclude games against the team.
RPI (adj)
NCAA volleyball bonuses/penalties: wins vs 1–25 +2 positions, 26–50 +1; losses vs 288–312 −1, vs 313+ or non-D1 −2; nonconference schedule ≥50% top-75 +2, ≥50% 263+ −2.
RPI futures
1,000 simulated seasons of the remaining schedule using the win model, with RPI recomputed each time.
Highlights
Gold = win vs current top 25; blue = win vs 26–50.
RPI by conference
Average RPI (with bonuses) of a conference's teams, how many sit in the top 25/50/100, and its nonconference D1 record, split by the opponent's current RPI rank. Bad losses = nonconference losses to teams ranked 151+.
