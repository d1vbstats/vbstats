Every league outside the Power 4 after three weekends of conference play, where the standings and our projection disagree, and the teams that played their way into the NCAA tournament conversation this week.

Twenty-one of these 28 leagues still have at least one team without a conference loss. The Sun Belt is the most crowded: James Madison, Georgia Southern, Troy, Arkansas State and Appalachian State are all 5-1, and no one wins the league in more than 36% of our runs.

Where the standings and the projection disagree

The projection rates teams on how they’ve played and who they still have to play, not on the record alone. In five leagues, the favorite isn’t the team in first:

  • Patriot. American is 8-0 and two games clear, but Lehigh (6-2) is the favorite, 53% to 40%. Lehigh is 90th in our power ratings and American is 117th, and Lehigh’s defense holds opponents to .134, fourth-best in D1.
  • Ivy. Cornell is the only unbeaten team at 5-0, but Yale (4-1) wins the league in 61% of runs to Cornell’s 18%. Yale is 47th in the RPI; Cornell is 107th.
  • Southland. UTRGV is 6-0 but wins the league only 20% of the time. Incarnate Word (5-1) is the favorite at 42%.
  • SWAC. Florida A&M leads at 6-1, but Alabama A&M (4-1) is the favorite at 66%.
  • American. Tulane and Tulsa are both 6-0. Tulsa is the favorite (44% to 32%) and is 50th in our power ratings to Tulane’s 66th.

Marquette beat Georgetown and Villanova at home and is now the Big East favorite (53%). It’s in the field in 98% of our runs, and it has a 45% chance at an at-large bid if it doesn’t win the league.

Eastern Kentucky (16-2, 6-0 UAC) swept Austin Peay and North Alabama. At 46th in the RPI, it now gets an at-large bid in 24% of runs if it slips in the league.

Cal Poly won at Long Beach State and is the Big West favorite. Utah Tech (6-0 Big Sky) beat Idaho State, one of the two teams a game behind it. Wright State climbed 19 spots in the RPI after beating Green Bay twice and is tied with Northern Kentucky atop the Horizon.

DePaul is the one in this group with a realistic at-large path (23%), even though it lost at home to Georgetown this week.

One team going the other way: Lipscomb is 42nd in the RPI and tied for first in the ASUN, but it makes the field in only 22% of our runs. The ASUN is likely a one-bid league, and West Florida is the favorite to win it.

The fine print

Standings count conference matches from Sept. 17 on. Field odds come from our simulations of every remaining D1 match (1,837 left). The ASUN, Big South, Ivy, NEC and Summit teams have played fewer conference matches than most, so those tables will move the most.
