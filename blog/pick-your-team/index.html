Pick your team

Star a favorite team and the site builds itself around it: highlighted in every table, first on the Teams tab, and lined up against its next opponent in Compare.

You can now set a favorite team. Open the Teams tab, pick your team and tap ☆ Make favorite. That’s it. No account and no sign-in. The favorite is saved in your browser, so it’s there the next time you visit on that device.

What changes

It’s highlighted everywhere, in your team’s colors. Your team’s row stands out in the National, RPI, Power and Bracketology tables, in the conference standings and results, on the player leaderboards and on the sideout charts. The highlight uses your school’s color, shaded so it reads in light and dark mode.

Teams opens on it. The Teams tab starts on your favorite instead of a blank pick. A link someone sends you still opens the team in the link.

Compare sets up the next match. The Compare tab puts your team against its next opponent on the schedule, with the home court already set. You get the projected result, the most likely score and the stat-by-stat matchup before the first serve. Pick any other teams and it works as before.

RPI and Trends start with your team. The cards at the top of the RPI tab show your team’s rank, its bonus or penalty and where it’s projected to finish. The Trends tab opens on your team’s ranking and stat history.

Conference stats and Scores follow along. Conference stats opens on your team’s league. On match days, your team’s match sits at the top of Scores.

Changing or clearing it

Your favorite shows as a small ★ chip at the top of every page. Tap the name to jump to your team, or the × to clear it. To switch teams, pick a new one on the Teams tab and make it your favorite.

Also new

Team search now knows nicknames. LSU, BYU, Ole Miss, Mizzou, UConn and SFA all find the right team, and so does “Miss. St.” with or without the periods.
